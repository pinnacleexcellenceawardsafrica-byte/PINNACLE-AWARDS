The Process

How Pinnacle Excellence Decides

Official judging and voting methodology will be published closer to the ceremony.

Nominate

02
Screen

Every nomination is reviewed for eligibility and completeness.

03
Shortlist

The Academy narrows each category to a final shortlist.

04
Judge

05
Public Vote

The public casts votes for their favourite nominees.

06
Crown

Winners are revealed live on the night.

Questions

FAQ

Official details will be announced by Pinnacle Excellence Awards Africa where not yet confirmed.

What are the Pinnacle Excellence Awards Africa?
Pinnacle Excellence Awards Africa is a digital awards platform celebrating excellence, talent and achievement across entertainment, creative, business, leadership, innovation and social impact across the African continent.
Who can be nominated?
Anyone whose work or achievement fits one of this year's published categories can be nominated by the public or the Academy.
How does voting work?
Each vote costs KSH 10, paid via M-Pesa at the point of voting. Once payment is confirmed, your vote is added to the nominee's tally for that category.
When is the ceremony?
The ceremony date will be announced by Pinnacle Excellence Awards Africa.
